2. Location Data (Critical for SOS Functionality)

2.1 What location data is collected

The Application collects your device’s precise GPS location when you grant permission.

2.2 When location is collected

ScenarioWhen it happens
ForegroundWhen the app is open and you have triggered an SOS alert (or related safety timer flow)
BackgroundAfter an SOS alert is triggered, location may continue to be used even if you close or minimize the app – until you manually end the SOS session

2.3 Why location is collected

The purpose is emergency safety: to include your location in alerts to your selected trusted contacts during an SOS event.

2.4 How location is delivered

Location coordinates and related information (such as a maps link) are sent to trusted contacts through push notifications using Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), which is operated by Google. That means location-related content transits Google’s systems as part of message delivery. The Service Provider does not intend to keep a separate long-term log of your coordinates on the Service Provider’s own servers for marketing purposes; however, Google’s infrastructure may process this data according to Google’s Privacy Policy and Firebase terms.

2.5 How you control location

  • You will be asked for location permission during first use.
  • You can choose: "While Using the App" or "Always Allow".
  • If you choose "Always Allow", the app can use location during an SOS alert even when the app is not in the foreground.
  • You can change this anytime in:
    Settings → Privacy → Location Services → OkoCrime

2.6 Disabling location

If you disable location access, SOS location sharing will not function as intended and trusted contacts may not receive your coordinates.

2.7 Battery impact

Continuous background location use during an active SOS alert may increase battery usage.

The Application includes a chat feature powered by Google’s generative AI (Gemini) through Firebase AI. When you send a message, your text input is transmitted to Google’s systems so the model can generate a response.

This AI feature is for entertainment and informational suggestions (e.g. books, podcasts, or media related to the app’s themes). It is not a substitute for professional, medical, legal, or emergency advice.

Important limitations:

• The Service Provider is not responsible for decisions you make based on AI output.
• Do not rely on AI for critical decisions (medical, legal, financial, or safety emergencies) without appropriate human help or emergency services.

If you have questions about AI features or data processing, contact the Service Provider at aksana.martysiuk@gmail.com.

4. Account Data, Friends, and Cloud Storage

Social and safety features use Firebase (including Firestore and Firebase Authentication). When you create an account or sign in, data such as the following may be stored in Firebase / on Google’s servers:

  • Your user identifier from your sign-in provider (e.g. Sign in with Apple or email-based auth, depending on what the Application supports).
  • Your public display name (username) and related profile fields used in the app.
  • Friend relationships, friend requests, and settings related to who may receive notifications.
  • FCM device tokens so the Application can send push notifications to your device.

Trusted contacts and friend-related information are therefore not limited to the device alone — they are synchronized through cloud storage so the features work across sessions and devices. Data is protected in transit using industry-standard encryption; Google operates the underlying cloud environment under its own security and compliance programs.

Support and voluntary contact: If you email the Service Provider (e.g. for support), the Service Provider may retain that correspondence as needed to assist you.

How this information is used:

  • To operate accounts, friends, SOS / notification flows, and the AI feature.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • Marketing communications only where permitted by law and, where required, with your consent.

Deletion: To request deletion of Firebase-held account data, contact aksana.martysiuk@gmail.com. You may also be able to delete your account from within the Application where that option is provided. Local data on your device is removed when you uninstall the app (see Section 7).
